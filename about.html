To BIM,
or not to BIM

A live, multiplayer classroom game: a client, design team and contractor weigh different tiers of workforce and digital tools (BIM vs CAD) across a building project — rolling for quality, time and profit.

The mechanics

Inside the game

A faithful digital rebuild of the original board game — every dice table, cost and formula preserved.

◈

Three parties, one project

A Client hires and pays a Design Team and a Contractor. Each has its own screen, incentives and profit.

✎

Workforce & tools

Choose Super / Senior / Junior talent and BIM vs CAD — each a coloured die with its own odds of quality and time.

🎲

Roll for outcomes

Deploy a combination and roll. Better odds cost more; the dice guide shows every probability.

💰

Bid, build, profit

Negotiate fees, hit the quality target, beat the clock — profit rewards quality and punishes delay.

📡

Live on phones

Everyone in a group shares one live board across their devices — no installs, just a link.

📊

Class dashboard

Project every group’s results live, then export the whole session to a spreadsheet.
